# The Presets page and a folder preset on three photos, then one undo.
# Run from a directory holding `presets/`, a folder of Lightroom .xmp
# presets with a `Creative` group of at least eight presets, on a
# make-fixture.sh folder:
#   lightphotos --drive presets.txt --drive-out out --catalog-dir cats photos
# `page` shows the rail's Presets icon lit directly above Sliders and the
# page listing its groups; `applied` has the Creative group open and the
# first three thumbnails changed; `undone` shows all three back as they were
# after one Cmd+Z; `loupe` shows the Presets icon in the Loupe's rail too.
# cats/default.lightphotos/presets.json lists the folder.
size 1200 800
idle
run {"id":"preset.add_folder","params":{"path":"presets"}}
idle
click-cell 0
click-cell 1 shift
click-cell 2 shift
# The rail's first icon, Presets.
click 1176 60
idle
shot page
# The Creative group's header, then its eighth row.
click 872 193
idle
click 928 361
idle
shot applied
state
key cmd+z
idle
state
shot undone
key e
idle
shot loupe
quit

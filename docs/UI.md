# UI

## Forms

Every form is built from `src/ui/form.rs`. That covers the modals, the right-panel Export form, and Develop's Crop and Masks tabs. `Form::new`, `section` and `row` lay out label and value rows; a side panel uses `Form::stacked`, which sets each label over its value. `form::title` heads a dialog, and a side panel page is `form::page_heading` over `form::page`. `form::segmented` is the control for a fixed single choice where one click should do it, such as Theme or crop Aspect; its segments wrap onto a grid when the row is too narrow, and it reports a click on the current segment too, so filter it when a repeat means nothing. Its segments are as tall as a footer button, so a side-by-side form puts it in `button_row` rather than `row`, which lowers the label to match. A choice that will grow, such as Language, is a list of radios. A long or open-ended list, such as Export Size or the Immich albums, is a ComboBox. A setting that is hidden for now sits behind a `SHOW_*` const in `src/app/mod.rs`, like `SHOW_AUTOTONE_CENTERING`, rather than being deleted. `form::hint` and `form::error` set helper and error text under a value. `form::dialog` opens a modal at `form::DIALOG_WIDTH` with the shared margin.

Buttons go through `form::footer`, never laid out by hand. A footer is a table of `form::Button { label, role, enabled }`, each with a `Role` of `Cancel`, `Primary` or `Danger`, and `footer` returns the role clicked. It sits against the right edge and orders the buttons for the platform: Cancel then the primary on macOS, the web and Linux, the primary then Cancel on Windows. Its buttons share a minimum width. A destructive confirm uses `Danger`. An action that belongs to one row, such as Immich's Connect, uses `form::button` with the same roles.

Spacing comes from the constants in `form.rs` through `font_size::px`, never from literal `add_space` numbers, so Alt+= and Alt+- scale it. The filled button colors are `primary_fill`, `primary_text`, `danger_fill` and `danger_text` in `theme::Palette`, and a test holds every theme to their contrast. A new fill needs a matching test.

A real page switch, such as Develop's Sliders, Crop and Masks, is a column of painted icons on the panel's right edge (`develop_panel::develop_rail`), with the page's name as hover text. It is not for a choice inside a form. New strings go in the `Strings` struct in `src/i18n.rs` and in every language's table under `src/i18n/` (English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French). A new CJK character needs `scripts/subset-cjk-font.sh` rerun.

The Develop Sliders page follows the selection (`App::develop_scope`). With nothing selected it says so in the histogram's place and every control is off, at its default. With one photo it shows that photo. With several it names no one photo: each slider is a row of step buttons (`«  ‹  0  ›  »`, five steps, one step, reset, one step, five steps of the slider's keyboard step; the reset button shows the slider's default, 50 for Vignette Midpoint) that move every selected photo from its own value, the curve is off, and Remove Chromatic Aberration shows a grey tick when only some photos have it. Ticking it measures the photos that lack it one at a time.

The Color Mixer shows one of Hue, Saturation and Luminance at a time, picked with a `form::segmented` row under its header, and gives way to the B&W Mix on a monochrome photo, as in Lightroom. Color Grading draws a row of four wheels (`ui::grade_wheel`): a press on a wheel's cell picks it, so its Hue, Saturation and Luminance sliders show under the row; a press or drag that starts on the disc sets its hue by angle and saturation by distance, and a double-click centres it. With several photos selected the wheels only pick. Each section's header folds it with a chevron (`form::collapsing_header`); the folded set is session state in `SliderView::collapsed`. `develop::Slider::shown` decides which rows draw, folded sections included, and the keyboard cursor walks only those.

The Masks page starts with one row of add buttons: `+ Linear` and `+ Radial` arm a draw tool, `+ Brush` arms the Brush for a new mask (K arms it for the picked brush mask), `+ Subject` and `+ Sky` add a detected mask at once (Lightroom's Select Subject and Select Sky). A detected mask has no handles on the photo; its detection runs in the background, and the button is greyed with the reason where this build has no detector (`scoring::detect::available`). Each mask's label is its kind and number, `Radial 1`, `Subject 2`; a detected mask shows a spinner while it detects and a `!` with the reason as hover text when it failed, and the picked one gets a `Retry` button beside Invert and Delete. While a detected or brush mask is picked, its coverage tints the photo red through the Loupe overlay. While the Brush is armed, its Size, Feather and Flow sliders and an Erase checkbox sit under the add buttons, and the pointer is the brush's ring, with a minus while it erases. Export waits for the selection's detections to land.

The rail's first icon, two overlapping circles directly above Sliders, opens the Presets page (`ui::presets`) in both the Grid and the Loupe, as the Lightroom app puts Presets above Edit; Sliders goes back. A search field heads it; under it My Presets, the library, comes first, then one folded header per preset group from the catalog's preset folders, and a search opens every group with a match. The list fills the panel and scrolls above a footer. A row applies its preset to the photos the page acts on, with no confirmation, since one Cmd+Z undoes the batch, and the page stays open, so one look after another can be tried; rows are off while nothing is selected. Library rows keep a `⋯` menu with Rename and Delete; a folder preset's row shows on hover what it can't carry. The footer has Save current as preset… (Cmd+Shift+N), Import from Lightroom… (desktop only), Add preset folder…, a Preset folders submenu with Remove for each, and a count of the files a scan skipped. Opening the page rescans the folders; in the browser that click also asks again for any folder whose access the browser withholds, which shows as a "Click to allow access" row until it is granted.

Copy Adjustment copies at once, with the items Copy Settings last kept. The cog beside it, Cmd+Shift+C and the menus' Copy Adjustment… open Copy Settings (`modals::copy_settings_modal`): a checkbox per `develop::copy::CopyGroup`, which shows a dash when only some of its items are on, over a checkbox per `CopyItem`, in two columns, with Check All and Check None. Crop, Straighten and Touch Up (`CopyItems::PER_COPY`) start unticked every time and are never remembered. Paste and preset apply both go through `develop::copy::merge`; a preset carries `CopyItems::LOOK`, the sliders, curve and colour work without Optics, Crop, Masks or Touch Up.

## Visual styling

Themes use layered studio chrome: `header` for the app header, `toolbar` for control bars and page headings, `panel` for working panels, and `window` for raised cards and dialogs. Photo cells and the Loupe backdrop stay neutral for color judgment. Shared widget visuals provide rounded secondary controls with distinct hover and pressed fills. Primary and destructive buttons use the form helpers, which add pointer feedback without changing their contrast-tested fills. Keep new surface colors in `theme::Palette` and include them in its contrast tests.

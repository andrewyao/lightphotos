# UI/UX review follow-up plan

This plan records every finding from the 2026-09-27 design review of the native macOS app and the web build. It then specifies the four fixes shipping now.

- **Fix now.** F1 (Esc quits the app behind a dialog you can't see), F2 (keys leak through confirm dialogs), F4 (the photo gets under half the window), and F5 (no File, Edit, View or Help menus, and incomplete shortcut help).
- **Waiting on a decision.** F3 (undo).
- **Backlog.** Everything else, listed in Appendix A.

## How to read this

Each finding has an ID, the evidence behind it, and a status. "Native" means it was seen in the macOS app, driven by System Events and CGEvent clicks. "Web" means the trunk build in headless Chromium. "Code" means it was read from source and not reproduced.

Each task under "Fix now" names its files, the data shape it changes, what counts as done, and how to verify it. The verification bar for every task is `cargo test && cargo build --release && cargo build --bins`, plus a native screenshot of the behavior.

## Fix now

### F1. Esc must never quit the app, and modal animations must finish without input

**Findings.**

- Egui's modal fade stalls until the next input event. Five seconds after it opened, "Quit LightPhotos?" was still almost fully transparent (native and web). The root cause is that `App::redraw` never reads `full_output.viewport_output[ROOT].repaint_delay`. `src/main.rs` sets `ControlFlow::Wait` whenever no decode or export is running, so egui's animation repaint requests are dropped.
- Esc steps out of the app one level per press and ends at a quit prompt, where Esc confirms the quit (`src/app/keys.rs:146`, `src/ui/modals.rs:137`). From a freshly opened grid, two presses of Esc quit the app (native).

**Change.**

- [ ] Honor egui's repaint delay. After `run_ui`, read the root viewport's `repaint_delay`. Zero means request a redraw. A finite delay feeds a `WaitUntil` deadline, the earlier of it and the existing poll delay. `Duration::MAX` changes nothing. This one change fixes every egui animation, including the modal fades, collapsing headers and tooltips.
- [ ] Remove the Esc path to quitting. Esc's chain ends at the grid with nothing selected, and a further Esc does nothing. Quitting stays on Cmd+Q, the window close button, and the File menu from F5. `exiting` already flushes the catalog and signal queues.
- [ ] Delete what then has no caller. That covers `pending_quit`, `quit_modal`, `UiAction::{ConfirmQuit, CancelQuit}`, the `quit_title` and `quit` strings in both languages, and their tests (**principle-subtract-before-you-add**).

**Done when.** From a freshly opened grid, any number of Esc presses leaves the app running. A modal opened by a key fades fully in with no further input.

### F2. A confirm dialog owns the keyboard while it is open

**Finding.** With "Move 20 photo(s) to the Trash?" open, pressing 2 rated the photo behind it (native). `handle_key` never checks `pending_bulk`, and the preset-delete modal has the same gap.

**Change.**

- [ ] Near the top of `handle_key`, after the text-field guards, gate on an open confirm (`pending_bulk`, or a pending preset delete). Esc cancels it, Return and keypad Enter confirm it, and every other key is swallowed.
- [ ] Style the destructive case. Title the bulk confirm with its action instead of "Confirm". Give Delete a danger-colored confirm button, following `theme::colors(ctx).danger`.
- [ ] Add a unit test in `src/app/keys.rs`. With a pending delete, a digit leaves the rating unchanged and Esc clears `pending_bulk`.

**Done when.** The native repro (select all, fn+⌫, press 2) leaves every rating unchanged, and Esc closes the dialog with all files intact.

### F4. The photo gets the space

**Findings (native).** The window always opens at 1100×800 points (`src/main.rs:141`) and never remembers its size. In the loupe, the left panel takes about 18% of the width to show one folder name, and the Develop panel clips Denoise at that height.

**Change.**

- [ ] Open at a larger default. Use 1440×900 points, clamped to 90% of the monitor the window opens on.
- [ ] Remember the window's size and position in `prefs` under the key `window`, as `x y w h` in logical points. Save it on resize and move, debounced to once per settle, or on `exiting`. Restore it at launch only when the saved rectangle still intersects a connected monitor, and fall back to the default otherwise.
- [ ] Hide the Folders panel in the loupe. In the loupe, the left panel shows only when its Metadata tab is active, so `I` toggles between no panel and Metadata. The grid keeps both tabs as they are today.

**Done when.** On a fresh launch the photo in the loupe is at least 60% of the window width. The window reopens at the size and place it closed at.

### F5. Native menus and complete shortcut help

**Findings.**

- On macOS the only menu is winit's default app menu (Hide, Quit). File, Edit, View and Help menus are missing, and those are where Mac users discover commands (native).
- The `?` overlay leaves out G, E, B, D, Cmd+O, Cmd+U, Cmd+Shift+U, Cmd+Shift+C, Cmd+Shift+Y, Alt+= and Alt+-, F6 and Tab, PageUp and PageDown, and double-clicking a slider to reset it. Its Settings row says "theme and language" but Settings also contains Auto Tone (code, native).

**Change.**

- [ ] Build the menu bar on macOS only, with `muda`, which is built for winit apps. Menu events wake the event loop through the existing `EventLoopProxy<UserEvent>`, the same way `macos_delegate` delivers Finder opens.
  - [ ] **LightPhotos.** About, Settings… (⌘,), Hide, Hide Others, Show All, Quit (⌘Q).
  - [ ] **File.** Open Folder… (⌘O), Export… (X).
  - [ ] **Edit.** Undo (⌘Z, Touch Up spots only until F3), Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All routed to text fields, Copy Settings (⇧⌘C), Paste Settings (⇧⌘Y).
  - [ ] **Photo.** Rate 0 to 5, Auto Tone (⌘U), Rotate Left and Right, Crop (C), Move to Trash.
  - [ ] **View.** Grid (G), Loupe (E), Info Panel (I), Before and After (Y), Zoom to Fit (⌘0), Actual Size (⌘1), Zoom In, Zoom Out, Bigger Text (⌥=), Smaller Text (⌥-).
  - [ ] **Window.** Minimize, Zoom.
  - [ ] **Help.** Keyboard Shortcuts (?).
  - [ ] Model the menu as one table of `(MenuCommand, label, accelerator)` rows. Each `MenuCommand` maps to an existing `App` method or `UiAction`, never to new behavior (**principle-model-the-domain**). Enable and disable items each frame from the same predicates the keys use, for example Paste Settings only when `has_copied_settings()`.
  - [ ] Show an accelerator in the menu only for chorded shortcuts (⌘, ⇧⌘, ⌥). Bare-letter keys like G and 0 to 5 stay app-handled, because an NSMenu single-key equivalent would fire inside text fields.
- [ ] Complete the help overlay in `src/i18n.rs` for both English and 中文. Add every missing row above. Change the Settings row to "Settings: theme, language and Auto Tone".
- [ ] Keep Linux, Windows and wasm unchanged. `muda` goes under `[target.'cfg(target_os = "macos")'.dependencies]`.

**Done when.** Every menu item performs its command in the native app. The Help menu opens the overlay. The overlay lists every binding in `handle_key` that isn't gated off by a `SHOW_*` flag.

## Waiting on a decision

### F3. Undo for edits and ratings

**Finding.** Cmd+Z undoes only Touch Up spots (`src/app/keys.rs:78`). Slider moves, Reset, Auto Tone, Apply Settings, presets and ratings have no undo. A single stray click on the Exposure track set +3.00 EV (native). Every bulk action opens a confirm modal to make up for this.

**Questions to settle.**

- Scope. Is it one app-wide history in Lightroom's style, or one history per photo?
- Granularity. Is a slider drag one step, and is a bulk action across 200 photos one step?
- Persistence. Should the history survive a restart? The sidecar's existing edit record could hold it.
- Follow-on. Once undo exists, the confirm modals for ratings, Auto Tone and Apply Settings could go, leaving one only for Delete.

## Appendix A. Backlog findings

Ordered by impact within each group.

**Trust and state.**

- A3. A photo that fails to decode shows the same "…" as one still loading (`src/ui/grid.rs:358`). In the web build, rawler panicked at `pumps.rs:40` on one ARW and the cell stayed "…" (web).
- A4. A filter that matches nothing shows a blank grid with no message. The title bar says "Grid (20 photos)" while the toolbar says "0 photos" (native).
- A5. Web on a Mac. The help lists Ctrl, but Cmd is what users press (web).

**Discoverability.**

- A6. Keys change meaning by mode with no on-screen mode indicator. `[` and `]` rotate or size the brush, O toggles the subject overlay or the spots, Space opens or zooms, and Up and Down move or zoom (code).
- A7. The delete key doesn't match the Mac keyboard. Only forward-delete trashes photos, and ⌫ does nothing. Mac convention is ⌘⌫ (native).
- A8. "Masks" is spot healing and has no instructions (native).
- A9. Hidden features still have live keys. C opens a crop mode that shows no instructions, no Done or Cancel, and no corner handles, while its tab is hidden. B and D work while the grouping tools are hidden (native, code).
- A10. The loupe shows no zoom level (native).
- A11. The ≥, = and ≤ filter mode is cryptic and takes two steps (native).

**Visual polish.**

- A12. Every unrated thumbnail shows five empty stars, and they overlap portrait photos (native).
- A13. The grid is left-aligned with a ragged right gutter. The selected cell is a heavy blue slab (native).
- A14. Amber focus outlines stack up, one on the Develop panel and one on a slider, and one frames the photo at all times (native).
- A15. The header row repeats the app name under the title bar and holds three small buttons. Two thin toolbars are stacked below it (native).
- A16. Opening the export form shifts the photo. The form has no JPEG quality or naming options (native).
- A17. Sliders are flat gray with no gradients on Temp and Tint, and labels sit on their own rows (native).
- A18. Filmstrip ratings show as three tiny dots (native).
- A19. The first click on an inactive window only activates it (native).
- A20. The landing page leaves half the screen empty and opens its tips with a warning. The web canvas shows a browser focus ring. The Settings theme order is Dark, Light, Medium (web, code).
- A21. The help overlay says "Cmd+" where Mac convention is ⌘, and it has no cue that it scrolls (native).
